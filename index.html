MailDesk

MailDesk is an email client for Windows. It connects from your PC directly to your mail provider over the standard mail protocols, IMAP and SMTP, and keeps your mail and your sign-in details on your PC.

  • Several accounts side by side, with one inbox for all of them.
  • Works with Gmail (with a Google app password), Outlook.com and Hotmail (with Microsoft sign-in), Yahoo, iCloud, and other providers that offer IMAP and SMTP.
  • Folders, search, attachments, and writing, replying to, and forwarding mail.
  • New-mail notifications, with verification codes that can be copied with one click.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no MailDesk account.
